Power Policy & Innovation Summit

The global transition towards sustainable energy systems and smart grid technologies demands advanced tools for system modeling, analysis, and optimization. Hence, shaping the future of Bangladesh's power industry requires leadership, smart planning, and well-planned policies.

To bridge this gap, the IEEE NSU Power & Energy Society (PES) Student Branch Chapter, in collaboration with the IEEE NSU Student Branch, IEEE NSU Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) Student Branch Chapter, IEEE NSU Industry Application Society (IAS) Student Branch Chapter, IEEE NSU Women in Engineering (WIE) Affinity Group, and the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE), North South University, is organising "Power Policy & Innovation Summit" — an interactive two-day National Flagship event bringing students, young innovators, industry leaders, experienced professionals and policymakers from all over the country together to collaborate, learn, and innovate.

Event Name: Power Policy Innovation & Summit
Event Date: 23 October and 31 October 2026
Venue: North South University
